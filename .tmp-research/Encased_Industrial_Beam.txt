'''Encased Industrial Beam''' is a mid-game component created by embedding [[Steel Beam|Steel]] in [[Concrete]]. It is used for [[Conveyor Belt Mk.4|Conveyor Belts Mk.4]] (and [[Conveyor Lift|Lifts]]), buildings, [[Heavy Modular Frame|Heavy Modular Frames]], [[Uranium Fuel Rod]]s and some [[Alternate recipes|alternate recipes]].

== Tips ==
* It is encouraged to automate this item with Alternate: Encased Industrial Pipe once available, as it consumes fewer Steel Ingots.
* Comparison between original recipes and alternate recipes for crafting 1 Encased Industrial Beam:

|-

Using alternate recipes, there is a trade-off between resource efficiency vs. Space and Power usage.
* This requires [[Iron Ingot|Pure Iron Ingot]], [[Steel Ingot|Solid Steel Ingot]], [[Concrete|Wet Concrete]] and [[EIB|Encased Industrial Pipe]].
