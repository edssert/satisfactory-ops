The '''Zipline''' is an early-game piece of hand [[equipment]] that can be used for navigating along [[Power Line]]s. Despite statements in pre-release teasers, it is not necessary for the Power Line to be powered, meaning it can be effectively used for exploration or crossing gaps.

== Obtaining ==
The Zipline tool is the first item in the game not to be "official FICSIT-sanctioned equipment",<ref name="yt-Bg2olZpAv_g@114">[https://youtu.be/Bg2olZpAv_g?t=114 YouTube - June 22nd, 2021 Livestream Snutt & Jace & Torsten Talk: Concept Art - Zipline]</ref> having been jury-rigged by the [[Pioneer]] in their off time from existing parts & technology such as the "newly-discovered [[Caterium]]",<ref name="yt-Bg2olZpAv_g@114" /> and the FICSIT-branded adhesive tape from the [[Nobelisk]].<ref name="yt-Bg2olZpAv_g@156">[https://youtu.be/Bg2olZpAv_g?t=156 YouTube - June 22nd, 2021 Livestream Snutt & Jace & Torsten Talk: Concept Art - Zipline]</ref>

== Usage ==
=== Equipment ===
While equipping a Zipline in the hand slot, approaching a [[Power Line]] and holding {{mouse|left}} or pressing {{mouse|right}} to toggle on/off uses the Zipline to travel along the Power Line. Once attached to the Zipline, the player will move forward in the direction the player is looking.

Ziplining can be stopped by letting go of {{mouse|left}} or pressing {{mouse|right}}. Ziplining will also end if the player bumps into anything.

It is possible to continue travelling between multiple Power Lines connected at a [[Wall Outlet]] or [[Power Tower]]. [[Power Tower Platform]]s are specifically designed for this purpose. The desired Power Line can be selected by looking in its direction, unless it exceeds the maximum turn angle of 60 degrees.

Ziplining on a level Power Line is just as fast as running with Blade Runners, 13.5 m/s. Traveling down a diagonal Power Line will speed up travel and vice versa, zipping up diagonal / almost vertical Power Lines will slow down to ~5 m/s. It is not possible to zip along vertical Power Lines.

Sprinting while ziplining speeds up the movement. The sprint key ({{key|left-Shift}} has to be pressed each time the game is restarted, and will be remembered if the [[Settings#Controls|Hold to sprint option]] is disabled (OFF).

In [[Patch 1.0|Version 1.0]] the particle effects while using Zipline were improved.<ref name="yt-0GfpAKAwkiQ">[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GfpAKAwkiQ YouTube - Satisfactory - Zipline ride to my SAM site]</ref> In [[Patch 1.2.0.0|Version 1.2]] the static electric effects while holding Zipline open were improved.<ref name="Reddit-1u8zs2y">[https://www.reddit.com/r/SatisfactoryGame/comments/1u8zs2y/i_noticed_another_new_animation_freom_12_on_top/ Reddit - I noticed another new animation freom 1.2 on top of the xeno zapper re-animation - Zipline Improved FX]</ref>

== Trivia ==
* If a Power Line is edited using a [[Online tools|save editor]] to make it longer than the in-game maximum of 300 meters, the Zipline is still usable along its entire length. Up to 500 meters have been tested.{{Citation needed}}
