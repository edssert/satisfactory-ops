🗺 1. Start: Choosing a Location

Recommended starting zone: Grass Fields

Flat terrain

Easy access to key resources

Great for large factory layouts



---

🛠 2. Progression by HUB Tiers

Tier 0–1: Basic Chains

🔹 Goal: Learn to automate simple products.

What to do:

Gather resources (iron, limestone, copper).

Build the HUB and Portable Miner.

Unlock Smelter → Constructor → Storage.

Create production lines for plates, rods, wires.


📌 Tip: Unlock biomass → biofuel for efficient early power.


---

Tier 2–3: Space Elevator & Logistics

🔹 Goal: Unlock advanced parts and logistics.

What to do:

Build the Space Elevator.

Craft Smart Plating for progression.

Learn Splitters and Mergers.

Unlock Assembler and Object Scanner.


📌 Production Chains:

Iron → Plate → Smart Plating

Copper → Wire → Cable



---

Tier 4–5: Oil and Transportation

🔹 Goal: Automate liquid production and vehicles.

What to do:

Locate oil deposits (use Vehicles or Zipline).

Build Refineries.

Produce plastic, rubber, fuel.

Set up tractors, truck stations, and roads.


📌 Important: Use the Packager to store liquids in containers.


---

Tier 6–8: Factories of the Future

🔹 Goal: Use nuclear power and advanced materials.

What to do:

Set up trains, stations, and signals.

Refine aluminum, composites, radioactive ores.

Build a Nuclear Power Plant – for stable energy.


📌 Don’t forget: safely manage radioactive waste!


---

📦 3. Factory Optimization

Tips:

Bus system – central belt lines run through your factory.

Modular design – each building produces one item, fully connected.

Multilevel layout – one floor per resource or function.



---

🚀 5. What to Do Next?

Stage Goal

🌍 Exploration Discover slugs, secrets, and alternate recipes
📡 M.A.M. Research alien items and tech
🔋 Energy Build biomass → fuel → nuclear power
🚆 Transport Set up trains and manage cargo routes
☢ Nuclear Unlock nuclear power and handle waste
🌌 Finale Complete all Space Elevator milestones

✅ A Detailed Beginner’s Guide to Satisfactory

A first-person factory-building simulator focused on automation and production on an alien planet.


---

🛠 1. What is Satisfactory?

Satisfactory is a 3D first-person game where you build automated factories on an alien planet, explore the environment, gather resources, automate production, and develop technologies.


---

🚀 2. Choosing a Starting Zone

When creating a world, you’ll be offered 4 starting biomes:

🌄 Grass Fields – Best for beginners: flat terrain and plenty of space.
🏜 Desert – Open terrain, but water can be an issue.
🌳 Northern Forest – Resource-rich but has less building space.
🧊 Rocky Desert – A balance of resources and difficulty.

📌 Recommended start – Grass Fields


---

🧱 3. First Steps After Landing

1. Build the HUB

This is the center of your base. Follow the marker and place the HUB.
Complete the first milestones in the HUB terminal.

2. Gather Essential Resources

Start with iron ore – your main resource in early stages.
Later you’ll need copper, limestone, coal, and oil.

3. Craft Tools

Build a Portable Miner – an automatic mining machine.
Craft inventory items using iron rods and iron plates.


---

⚙ 4. Automation: Where to Begin

1. Build a Basic Production Chain

Miner Mk.1 → Conveyor Belt → Smelter → Conveyor Belt → Constructor

2. Example: Iron Rods

The Miner extracts iron → the Smelter produces ingots → the Constructor makes rods.

3. Connect Power

Build a Biomass Generator.
Craft fuel from leaves/wood → biomass → biofuel.
Connect power using Power Poles and Wires.


---

📈 5. Progression

Your goal is to unlock Tiers in the HUB terminal:

Tier 0–1 – Learn to automate simple production.

Tier 2–4 – Unlock vehicles, trains, and oil.

Tier 5–8 – Advanced chains, Space Elevator, aluminum, and nuclear power.


📦 Space Elevator

Be sure to build it – it’s necessary for tech progression.
Send special parts (like Smart Plating) to unlock new tiers.


---

🧠 6. Tips for Beginners

✅ Plan ahead. You’ll have hundreds of conveyors and buildings – don’t build too close together.

✅ Learn hotkeys:

F – Dismantle mode

R – Rotate build mode

Mouse Scroll – Rotate objects

Ctrl – Snap to grid


✅ Watch your power. If you run out of fuel, everything stops.

✅ Use the M.A.M. (research bench):
Find and build it.
Scan artifacts, berries, and Power Slugs – they unlock upgrades and recipes.

✅ Use Power Slugs. They create Overclock Shards to speed up machines.


---

🌍 7. Explore the World

Exploration brings new resources and technologies.
Beware of wild creatures (insects, spitters, etc.).
Use the Xeno-Zapper or Blade to defend yourself.


---

🧪 8. What to Do Next

Set up full automation for all basic resources.

Reach the oil tier – unlock plastic, rubber, fuel.

Move on to trains, drones, nuclear power.

Optimize your production chains → goal: reach the Space Elevator finale.



---

🔚 Conclusion

Satisfactory is about creativity, logistics, and automation.
You set your own goals: build a beautiful megafactory, explore the world, or optimize every chain — it’s up to you.
