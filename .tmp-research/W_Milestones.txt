'''Milestones''' are the primary method of progression in the game, allowing the [[pioneer]] to unlock new [[buildings]], [[:Category:Items|recipes]], [[:Category:Equipment|equipment]], and more. They can be completed at the [[HUB Terminal]] by inputting the specified resources. To do so, move near the terminal (the PC-shaped booth inside the [[HUB]]), interact {{key|E}} with it, then {{key|Ctrl}}+{{mouse|left}} the required material in your [[inventory]]. 

Sets of milestones are grouped into tiers. At the start of a new game session, only Tier 0 is available (unless [[Onboarding]] is skipped, which will skip Tier 0 as well). Completing Tier 0 unlocks Tiers 1 and 2, and completing phases of the [[Space Elevator]] unlocks higher tiers.

Parts inserted into the HUB Terminal cannot be taken back.

== Tier 0: Onboarding ==
Tier 0 focuses on upgrading the [[HUB]]. Unlike milestones in later tiers, all the HUB upgrades must be completed in sequential order. Each milestone changes the HUB's appearance as well.

Before HUB Upgrade 1, only the following is available:
* [[Craft Bench]] recipes: {{ItemLink|Iron Ingot}}, {{ItemLink|Iron Plate}} and {{ItemLink|Iron Rod}}
* [[Equipment Workshop]] recipe: {{ItemLink|Xeno-Zapper}} (appears in the Codex, but can't be crafted before the Equipment Workshop itself is unlocked in HUB Upgrade 1)
* Buildings: {{ItemLink|The HUB}} and {{ItemLink|Craft Bench}}
* [[Resource Scanner]]: {{ItemLink|Iron Ore}}

Several [[Onboarding]] steps have to be completed before the HUB can be constructed and its terminal accessed to begin completing milestones.
