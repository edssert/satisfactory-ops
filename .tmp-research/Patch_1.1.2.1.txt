== Intro ==
Hi Pioneers!

It’s that festive time of the year once again and now it’s double as FICS*MAS also will be available for the first time for all the new console players we have on PS5 and Xbox so everyone can enjoy the event from the 1st of December until the 18th of January

https://youtu.be/C66pqwPOqTU

If you’re not feeling festive but would still like to continue enjoying Satisfactory, remember that the event can be disabled from the main menu by going to Options > Gameplay > Disable Seasonal Events

For this year, given there’s many new players we wanted to give them an opportunity to experience the event so there’s no major changes to the previous year other than small bug fixes

However! For both PC and Console players alike, we’re running a competition starting now until the 9th of January 2026

You can join in the competition and show your FICS*MAS spirit by building the ultimate FICS*MAS workshop/Santa’s Grotto in Satisfactory, check the reddit post below for more details:

https://www.reddit.com/r/SatisfactoryGame/comments/1pb7k7j/ficsmas_competition_2025/

There’s some minor bug fixes in here as usual so if you find anything wrong with the game or FICS*MAS let us know over at our QA Site https://questions.satisfactorygame.com/  We read both your PC and Console feedback every day

And If you didn’t know Satisfactory is out on PS5 or Xbox you can check the links below:
https://store.playstation.com/concept/10008447
https://www.xbox.com/games/store/satisfactory/9npr2cg6s95q

Merry FICS*MAS everyone ❤️ 

==Bug fixes==

** Please note that FICSMAS Gifts will stop falling from the sky after building 10 FICSMAS Gift Trees, this is intended and not a bug
** The FICSMAS Holiday Event MAM research will not be reset once completed
