The '''Space Elevator''' is a special building used to complete phases of Project Assembly by supplying it with [[:Category:Project Assembly parts|Project Assembly Parts]]. The first three deliveries unlock two higher Tiers of [[Milestones]], while the fourth one unlocks the 9th and final Tier and the fifth delivery launches Project Assembly and unlocks the [['Employee of the Planet' Cup]] in the [[AWESOME Shop]].

It is visible from all parts of the [[world]], making it a useful landmark for navigation.

== Usage ==
The Space Elevator must be constructed to unlock [[Milestones#Tier 3|Tier 3 and above]]. Only one Space Elevator at a time can be placed in a world.

=== Dismantling and relocating ===

The Space Elevator can be dismantled and relocated without losing progress at any time. When relocated, after the Space Elevator is rebuilt, the Project Assembly platform will move from its former location to align itself with the Space Elevator's new position.<ref name="Reddit-1jfwns9">[https://www.reddit.com/r/SatisfactoryGame/comments/1jfwns9/moving_space_station_video/ Reddit - Moving Space Station - Video]</ref>

=== Project Assembly ===
Project Assembly Parts can be manually supplied from the inventory {{key|tab}}, or with connected conveyors. Once an item has been loaded into the elevator, it cannot be retrieved manually, although dismantling the Space Elevator with the [[Build Gun]] will return all loaded items.

Once the required items are fully satisfied, use the mouse and drag the handle down, 2 times (Load → Seal → Send). When sent, an animation will play where the cargo platform will be ejected up into space, gliding along the central rail. After a while, it will come back down and dock in its default position.

If the package is sealed, but not sent, and the UI is closed, the handle will have to be re-pulled again (Load → Seal).

In a multiplayer game, the Space Elevator and its progress is shared among all players. The handle in the UI doesn't sync between players.

=== Project Assembly launch (ending) ===
{{Spoilerbox|Completing Phase 5 of Project Assembly initiates the ending, where a specific sequence of tasks must be performed through the Space Elevator display. When complete, Project Assembly can be launched by pulling a lever one final time, playing a special animation from the Space Elevator.

The elevator assembles a huge ship, which flies off and [[Ballistic Warp Drive|blasts]] open a wormhole before flying through it. Upon completion of this sequence, ADA will speak again, congratulating the player as the credits begin. From this point on, the elevator repositions itself to form the [[FICSIT]] Logo, and the console will permanently display the message "Project Assembly Launched. Pioneer purpose concluded."

=== Total number of parts needed ===
Most project parts from later phases also need project parts from earlier phases, e.g. the {{ItemLink|Modular Engine}} is needed 500 times in Phase 3 and 625 times in Phase 4 (for crafting 250 {{ItemLink|Thermal Propulsion Rocket|Thermal Propulsion Rockets}}). This means that the total number of parts needed in every phase is higher than the initial phase requirements for that part (e.g. 1,625 {{ItemLink|Modular Engine}} in total vs. just the 500 for completing Phase 3).

== Tips ==
* Although it has two terminals in its model, the player can interact {{Key|E}} with the Space Elevator from any angle.
* If you are placing a Space Elevator on a foundation platform, you can get centering guidelines by placing observation towers at two cardinal, non-aligned compass points (N/E, N/W, S/E, or S/W, but not N/S or E/W) on your platform, then stand on one of the towers, you will get alignment guidelines to place the center of the elevator in the center of the platform.
* An easier alternative is to find the center of the platform, look directly down, place a hologram under your feet, lock it with {{Key|H}}, move away, confirm that the elevator is facing the right way and build it.
* Due to the high cost of project parts, early-stage pioneers might want to prevent overproducing them, in order to save up on parts that are harder to manufacture. Later on, the project parts can be a good source of [[AWESOME Sink]] points.
** Many early parts are used to craft later parts, so extras can usually be saved to speed up production later on.
* Strategic use of [[Somersloop|Somersloops]] can be used to ease Project Part production - by amplifying the machine making each stage of Project Part, one Ballistic Warp Drive will only cost 1.25 Smart Plates, instead of the usual 5.
* In multiplayer, all pioneers currently online will also receive the relevant achievement for a phase being completed, so it is possible for multiple pioneers to get every achievement related to Project Assembly in a single shared save. However, pioneers who are offline when a phase is completed will not receive these achievements upon logging back in.

== Trivia ==
* After each delivery, the Space Elevator will ascend to the sky for a few minutes then descend back to the lower level.
* Only the anchors of the Space Elevator are constructed by the pioneer, which explains its rather cheap price. When built, the anchor grabs the shaft, which is dropped from space.
* The delivery mechanism of the Space Elevator does have collision maps, so if the player managed to work their way up to the top, they would be able to walk around on it. However, this collision area is very inaccurate, meaning that you won't be walking directly on the platform.
* The Project Assembly Station can be seen in game, above the Space Elevator, and visually builds new components with each delivery.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W_lmP8jYVLs&t=124 YouTube - Satisfactory Reveal Trailer E3 2018]</ref>
* The elevator's shaft texture is very low-quality above the platform. Below the  platform, the shaft has a much higher-quality texture. This part of the elevator has blinking lights that flash in an upward-traveling pattern.
* It is possible to complete the last phase within 30 hours. In speedrun community, it is often referred as 'Npackage% speedrun' where N can be 1, 2, 3 or 4, and '1' is omitted for the first phase.
* The internal name of the Space Elevator is "Spelevator".<ref name="yt-otbc-OOuZQc">[https://youtu.be/otbc-OOuZQc YouTube - August 22nd, 2023 Livestream - Snutt Talk: Internal name for Space Elevator]</ref>
* An early concept had considered using the Space Elevator for [[AWESOME Sink|sinking items]], but this was discarded due to concerns about getting all the resources to one central location, along with wanting to make the Awesome Sink more useful.<ref name="yt-fbCEIVUNDe0">[https://youtu.be/fbCEIVUNDe0 YouTube - June 8th, 2020 Livestream - Q&A: How about repeatable Space Elevator shipments to make more use of it?]</ref>
* Despite having 6 conveyor inputs, none of the phases require more than 4 different inputs. No other building in the game has such redundant inputs that no recipe would fully utilize.
* The space elevator and the project assembly at the top end have a physical existence in the game world and can be reached by the player, provided the player is okay with being dead when they arrive since the project is in the upper kill zone. Within the game world, the height of the space elevator cable is 55km. The project assembly is approximately 24km in diameter and altitude at the top is 33km.
* In reality, since it is the anchor for a space elevator cable system, the project assembly must exist very close to geosynchronous orbit. If the game's planet is roughly equivalent in mass to Earth then the project's distance must be roughly 36,000 km from the surface (not the 55km of the physical cable in the game world). Since the project occupies roughly 40 degrees of arc in the sky, given the fixed distance, its diameter can be calculated at about 26,000 km, or a little more than twice the diameter of the earth.
** From here, further inferences can be made about the project's colossal mass. For example, if we suppose that the project is about 10% as tall as it is wide, is 75% empty space, and the remaining space is solid steel, then this gives the project about 36% the mass of Earth's moon.
** Given the known circumference of the project (about 82,000 km) and observing the trams moving around its outer edge (roughly 35 degrees of arc in 5 seconds), we can determine that they move at a speed of roughly 34 million km/h, which is nearly Mach 28,000 or 3% the speed of light. Since they appear to start and stop essentially instantaneously, a person inside such a tram would experience about 2 million G's of acceleration, which is probably not great for the human body.
