'''Somersloops''' are alien artifacts found throughout the [[world]] used for [[production amplification]] and to construct {{BuildingLink|Alien Power Augmenter|Alien Power Augmenters}}.

They emit a red glow and unusual particles, as well as a pulsating sound that fluctuates in volume. They will occasionally emit the sound of a voice, encouraging the player to harvest it.

== Obtaining ==
Somersloops, like [[Mercer Sphere|Mercer Spheres]] can be found while exploring, typically in hidden or hard-to-reach places, usually guarded by enemies and surrounded by [[Resource Node#Resource Deposit|Uranium Deposits]], [[Spore Flower]]s, [[Cracked boulder]]s or [[Gas Pillar]]s.

There are a total of 106 Somersloops in the world as of [[Patch 1.0]].

== Usage ==
=== Production amplification ===
{{Main|Production amplifier}}
Somersloops are used for [[production amplification]] ("slooping"). Inserting Somersloops into select production buildings (once production amplification is researched in the [[MAM]]) increases the building's output without increasing the input costs, but doing so massively increases the machine's power consumption by up to 4x. The maximum boost is +100% for doubled output in total.
