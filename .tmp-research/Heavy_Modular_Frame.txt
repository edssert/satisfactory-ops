== Tips ==
Heavy Encased Frame (HEF) [[Alternate Recipes|Alternate Recipe]] lowers belt load from over 239/m [[Screw]]s to under 21/m [[Concrete]] and pairs well with other Alternate Recipes: [[EIB|Encased Industrial Pipe]] and [[Steel Ingot|Solid Steel Ingot]]. Both conserve [[Coal]], improving logistics when upgrading factories. Heavy Flexible Frame (HFF) Alternate Recipe increases belt load to 390/m Screws and has similar name to get mixed up when researching for HEF.
