'''Hard Drives''' are special items obtained from [[Crash Site]]s used to unlock [[Hard Drive#Alternate recipes|alternate recipes]], as well for as select [[MAM]] researches.

== Obtaining ==
=== Crash Sites ===
There are 118 [[Crash Site]]s scattered around the [[world]], each containing a Hard Drive. Therefore, 118 Hard Drives can be obtained.

Crash Sites can be located using [[Object Scanner]] after [[Quartz Research|Radio Signal Scanning]] has been researched in the [[MAM]]. There are [[Crash_Site#Retrieving_the_hard_drive|unlock requirements and environmental threats]] that must be met or overcome before you can retrieve a specific Hard Drive.

The Hard Drive inside a Crash Site must be retrieved before it can be dismantled.

=== AWESOME Shop ===
After unlocking the Particle Enrichment milestone in [[Tier 8]], Hard Drives can be purchased at the [[AWESOME Shop]] for 100 {{ItemLink|FICSIT Coupon|Coupons}}.

=== FICSMAS ===
Additionally, one Hard Drive can be received during the [[FICSMAS#Rewards|FICSMAS]] seasonal event from the Advent Calendar.

=== Scanning ===
Each Hard Drive can be scanned in the [[MAM]] and results in a choice of one of two alternate recipes, chosen from the pool [[#alternate recipes|below]], as well as two [[pioneer]] [[inventory]] expansions (see [[#Hard Drive library|§ Hard Drive library]]).

There are a total of 106 alternate recipes available, each requiring one Hard Drive to be unlocked. The only exception is Distilled Silica, which is unlocked alongside Quartz Purification. Therefore, 105 Hard Drives are used to unlock alternate recipes, and two extra for both inventory expansions, resulting in a total of 107.

== Alternate recipes ==
[[File:Hard Drive Library.png|thumb|320px|Example of choices for alternate recipes granted by analyzing a Hard Drive<ref name="yt-oQEZC_tIoP8@150">[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQEZC_tIoP8&t=150s YouTube - We decided to REDO this Feature - Hard Drive Library]</ref>]]
Alternate recipes use different input materials than the original, balancing factors such as raw material consumption, space, power, simplicity, and material availability. Alternate recipes cannot be considered upgrades.

Two alternate recipes are chosen at the beginning of the scan and revealed to the player after the 10 minute research concludes. If the player chooses not to select an alternate recipe, they can access the selection menu at a later time via the hard drive library. The selected alternate recipe is unlocked for use immediately. Alternate recipes can be only used in factory buildings, not in the [[Craft Bench]]. Therefore, parts like [[Compacted Coal]] cannot be hand-crafted.

For an alternate recipe to appear in the selection, certain prerequisites (Milestone, MAM Research, or another alternate recipe) have to be met. If there is no alternate recipe available from the current prerequisites, the MAM will refuse to scan the Hard Drive and print "no rewards currently available". Unlocking more prerequisites during an ongoing scan won't affect the choices presented at the end of the scan, as these choices are determined at the beginning of the scan.

In the table below, the prerequisites are the conditions that are required for a recipe to be able to appear in the research result choices. If no prerequisite is displayed, the recipe is available immediately in the recipe pool (but you still need the MAM to scan the Hard Drive). Note that some alternate recipes may require the player to unlock a new building so that they could be automated.

=== Hard Drive library ===
Scanning a Hard Drive in the MAM stores it in the Hard Drive library.<ref name="yt-oQEZC_tIoP8@150"></ref> The library allows storing scanned Hard Drives without immediately choosing a reward, allowing players to delay their choices and potentially choose their rewards more strategically.

Storing multiple Hard Drives in the library narrows down the pool of rewards for newly scanned and re-rolled drives, as rewards will not repeat. This increases the likelihood of obtaining a desired reward, as the pool is reduced by two options, instead of just one (the selected reward).

It is possible to re-roll the presented rewards for each Hard Drive once. This means being able to choose from four unique rewards from a single Hard Drive. Re-rolling is also limited by other Hard Drives in the library, just like scanning a Hard Drive anew.

== Important notes ==
[[File:QuickReference.jpeg|thumb|1221x1221px|Quick reference for alternate recipes, created by Reddit user [https://www.reddit.com/user/oldshavingfoam oldshavingfoam].]]
* The Hard Drive research is repeatable, as long as there are remaining alternate recipes to unlock.
** Each alternate recipe can only be unlocked once, it will never appear in the selection screen again once it has been selected.
* The game does not list which alternate recipes are unlocked in one place, the [[Codex]] has to be searched for them.

== Tips ==
* Research within the MAM continues regardless of a MAM being present on the map, also multiple MAMs may be constructed. Therefore, when a Hard Drive has been acquired, the player may build a MAM at the crash site, load the Hard Drive in, and then remove the MAM, continuing their search for further drives without building a backlog of Hard Drives to research (or, if they acquire drives faster than a rate of one per 10 minutes, can significantly reduce the amount of drives in their backlog). Only one Hard Drive can be researched at a time, meaning multiple MAMs cannot be used to research more than one Hard Drive at a time.
